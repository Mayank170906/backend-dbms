Codebase: Signals

Every signal receiver in the project, and the apps.py hooks that register them.

Three apps wire signal receivers, each via an apps.py ready() hook importing that app's signals.py (the standard Django pattern — signals must be imported somewhere for their @receiver decorators to register).

apps/tasks/signals.py

  • post_save + post_delete on Task → invalidate_stats_on_task_change (calls apps.projects.caching.invalidate_project_statistics).
  • post_save on Task → update_search_vector_on_task_save.
  • post_save + post_delete on TaskAssignment → invalidate_stats_on_assignment_change.

apps/projects/signals.py

post_save on Project → update_search_vector_on_project_save.

apps/comments/signals.py

post_save on Comment → update_search_vector_on_comment_save.

The other 6 apps (accounts, activity, notifications, organizations, workflows, analytics) use a plain AppConfig with no ready() override and register no signals.
