Codebase: URLs

The complete URL map: config/urls.py, config/api_router.py, and WebSocket routing.

Routing is centralized, not spread per-app: config/api_router.py registers every ViewSet on one DefaultRouter, mounted at /api/v1/ from config/urls.py. Only accounts has its own urls.py (for the non-ViewSet auth endpoints).
