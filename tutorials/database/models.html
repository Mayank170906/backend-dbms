Database: Models

Business purpose per model — full field tables live on Codebase → Models.

This page is deliberately short: the exhaustive field-by-field tables already live on Codebase → Models and aren't repeated here. What follows is the "why" for each table.

ModelBusiness purpose
UserAn account. The root of every foreign key that means "who did this."
OrganizationThe tenant boundary — every other table's data ultimately belongs to exactly one of these.
MembershipWho belongs to an organization, and with what role.
Team / TeamMembershipAn optional sub-grouping of users within an organization.
Workflow / WorkflowState / WorkflowTransitionAn organization-defined pipeline a project's tasks move through — configurable data, not a hardcoded status enum.
ProjectA unit of work within an organization, optionally bound to a workflow.
ProjectMembershipWho can work on a project, and with what role.
ProjectExportA tracked, asynchronously-generated CSV export job and its resulting file.
TaskA unit of trackable work inside a project.
LabelA project-scoped tag applied to tasks.
TaskAssignmentWho is responsible for a task (many-to-many, through table).
CommentDiscussion attached to a task, with @mention notifications.
ActivityLogAn immutable audit trail of everything meaningful that happened.
NotificationA per-user inbox entry pointing at whatever triggered it.
