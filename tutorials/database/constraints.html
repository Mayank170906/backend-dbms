Organization.slugfield-level unique=True
User.emailfield-level unique=True
Label.colorRegexValidator(r"^#[0-9A-Fa-f]{6}$") — must be a 6-digit hex color

Why Project.slug is scoped per-organization rather than global: two unrelated tenants both naming a project "backend" is normal and shouldn't collide — project URLs are already scoped under their organization, so global uniqueness would be an artificial constraint serving a URL scheme the app doesn't use.
