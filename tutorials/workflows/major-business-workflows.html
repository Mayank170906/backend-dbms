Major Business Workflows

The task lifecycle end to end, naming the exact service method behind each step.

StepTriggerService methodSide effects
1. CreatePOST /tasks/TaskService.create_taskActivityLog entry
2. Labellabels field on create/updateTaskService.add_labelrejects cross-project labels
3. AssignPOST /tasks/{id}/assign/TaskService.assignActivityLog entry, Notification to assignee
4. Comment / mentionPOST /tasks/{id}/comments/CommentService.add_commentActivityLog entry, Notification per real mentioned member
5. TransitionPOST /tasks/{id}/transition/WorkflowService.transition_taskActivityLog entry, Notification to other assignees
6. ExportPOST /projects/{id}/export/export_project_tasks_csv (Celery)ProjectExport row updated, export_ready Notification

Every step 1–5 above is wrapped in @transaction.atomic, so the "write plus its audit trail plus its notification" always happens together or not at all.
